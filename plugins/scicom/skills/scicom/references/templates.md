# Templates

Fill in the brackets. Delete whatever does not apply. Anything unknown becomes `[CHECK: …]`.

## Fact sheet (always first)

```
Finding (one plain sentence):
Evidence: peer-reviewed / preprint / conference · theory / simulation / experiment / observation / trial / prototype
Scale (sample, devices, duration):
What is new compared with earlier work:
Limitations and conditions:
Stage and realistic next steps (only what the researchers say):
People (name, role, affiliation):
Partners and funders (with required acknowledgement):
Paper (title, journal, date, DOI or link); embargo:
Visuals available (credit, licence):
```

## Message Box (COMPASS)

```
Issue (the broad topic):
Problem (the specific problem or question):
So what (why the audience should care):
Solutions (what the research offers or suggests):
Benefits (what improves if it works):
Main message in one sentence:
```

## Press release

```
[LABELS: peer-reviewed | experiment | devices]
EMBARGOED UNTIL [hh:mm time zone, day month year]

[Headline: finding in plain words, active verb, about 12 words at most]
[Subhead: why it matters / key context]

[City], [date] – [Lede: who found what, and why it matters, in 35–40 words at most.]

[Context: the problem, and what was known before.]

[How they did it: method in plain words, with one concrete detail.]

"[Approved quote from the lead researcher: meaning or motivation, not a repeat of facts]," says [name, role, institution].

[Implications and realistic next steps.]

[Limitations and conditions.]

[Optional: second approved quote from a partner or independent expert.]

[Credits: collaborators, institutions, funders and the required acknowledgement.]

Publication: [authors], '[title]', [journal], [date]. DOI: [doi]

Media contact: [name, phone, email] · Researcher available: [dates, languages]
Images: [link] · Caption, credit, licence, alt text
About [organisation]: [boilerplate]
```

## News item (website)

```
Headline (what happened / what was found)
Standfirst (one or two sentences: why it matters)
Opening paragraph: the news in people terms
Context and the people behind it, with an approved quote
How it was done (short)
What's next, plus limitations
Links: paper, team page, related news · Image with caption, credit and alt text
```

## News tip (for selected journalists)

```
Subject: [the finding in 8–10 words]
[Two or three sentences: what was found, why it matters, why now.]
[Paper link; embargo if any; researcher availability; visuals.]
[Your name and contact.]
```

## Media advisory

```
MEDIA ADVISORY – [what]
What: [event, demonstration, photo opportunity]
Who: [speakers, guests]
When: [date, time]
Where: [address, room, access instructions]
Why it matters: [one or two sentences]
Interviews, photos and filming: [possibilities and restrictions]
Registration and contact: [name, phone, email]
```

## Pitch email to one journalist

```
Subject: [the news, specific]
Hi [name],
[Why this fits what they cover: one sentence.]
[What was found and why their readers will care: two sentences.]
[Why now: publication date or embargo, a news hook.]
[What you offer: interview, data, visuals, lab visit, exclusive?]
[Link. No attachments.]
[Name, role, phone]
```

## Social media set

```

[A limitation or the next step]
[Credits and tags]
[Link] [1–3 hashtags]
```

**Thread:** see `social-media.md` (six posts: finding, problem, method, result, limits, credits and link).

**Instagram carousel:**

```
1. Hook   2. Problem   3. What we did   4. Finding   5. Why it matters   6. Credits / where to read more
```

**Short-video script (30–60 seconds):**

```
0–2 s hook | 2–15 s problem | 15–40 s finding with visual | 40–55 s why it matters | end: name, affiliation, link
```

## Communication plan (OASIS)

```
Objectives: organisational goal → behavioural goal (who should do what) → communication objectives
Audience insight: who, what they know and believe, where they get information, barriers, trusted messengers
Strategy and ideas: main message (Message Box), messengers, channels, formats, timing
Implementation: timeline, owners, approvals, budget, risks and mitigations
Scoring: indicators per objective (outputs, outtakes, outcomes), baseline, when to measure
```

## Stakeholder table

```
| Stakeholder | Interest | Power | What they want | What we want | Channel | Message | Owner |
```

## Policy brief

```
Title: key finding or implication
Key messages: 3–5 bullets
The issue: why now, for whom
What the evidence shows: plain language, one visual, level of certainty
Options (with consequences and trade-offs) OR recommendations
Implications and next steps
Authors, contact, date, sources
```

## Lay summary (150–250 words)

```
The question: …  What we did: …  What we found: …  Why it matters / what's next: …  (one sentence on limitations)
```

## Op-ed outline

```
News hook → argument (one sentence) → evidence 1–3 → strongest counter-argument and answer → call to action → byline and disclosure
```

## Holding statement (crisis)

```
What we know: [facts only]
What we are doing: [actions]
Who is affected and our concern for them: [empathy]
What people should do: [if relevant]
When we will update next: [time]
Contact: [spokesperson]
```
